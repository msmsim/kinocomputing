The alternative paradigms tracker

This is a living version of the round-up of alternative computing paradigms, which stays as its author wrote it. Every two weeks the lab looks at each paradigm again and asks four things: what has actually been measured since last time (results, not funding rounds), who is doing the work, what an outsider can get their hands on, and what a one-person lab could test. Each paradigm has a short entry, updated on every pass, and this page quotes the entries as they stand, so you can see which have moved. A quote is a snapshot: when an entry changes, this page is republished.

The first pass began with the incumbent stack, the thing every alternative is measured against, and has now started on the paradigms that let physics compute. The rest are listed by name until their turn comes.
