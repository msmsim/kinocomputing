Physics computing as repair, body computing as new ground

Two families of alternative computing sit differently in World Machines theory, on our reading. "Let physics compute" (analog, photonic, thermodynamic chips) is mostly pitched as doing the digital computer's job more cheaply: a repair for Modernity in its declining Dusk, judged by the old machine's yardstick. "Let bodies compute" (living tissue, organoids, embodied systems) reads closer to Divergence, where results are local by nature.

How sure: an untested idea, weakened by adversarial review. The bodies half is partly true by definition, and the yardstick half rests on thin evidence. The working analysis.
