Today's computing stack straddles two world machines

In World Machines theory, Modernity, which pulls history toward one destination, is in its declining Dusk, and Divergence, where outcomes depend on local conditions, is in its Day. On our reading, digital computing's chips-and-scaling layer looks like late Modernity: frontier training costs up 2.4× a year, a concentrated chip supply. But energy is only 2–6% of that cost, and ordinary market economics explains the concentration just as well. The idea that AI's deployment belongs to Divergence has no support yet.

How sure: an untested idea, weakened by adversarial review. The working analysis.
