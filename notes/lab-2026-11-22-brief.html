Lab brief: 22 November 2026

Tonight the lab tested whether the way a chip's noise is correlated across space helps a rain forecast, scored over blocks about 32 km square instead of pixel by pixel. Over those blocks the chip-native forecast closed about half the gap between a plain noise ensemble with matched spread and the standard method, STEPS, on one patch of radar data; pixel by pixel the same comparison closed about 2%. The average hides a split: at one of three forecast times the chip was well short of STEPS, and at the other two it beat it. An adversarial check found this too thin for more than a claim at "real-data", so it is published as one, narrowed to that wording: the claim, and the updated window run. The pixel-level result stands: the chip closes 16% of the gap, short of half.
