AI accelerators: who wins depends on the model and precision

Chips stripped to what neural networks need (TPUs, Cerebras, Groq): less general, still digital at heart.

Where it stands. The cleanest recent comparison is a benchmark run by SemiAnalysis, InferenceX,. Google's TPU v7 beats Nvidia's GB300 rack by 1.85 times per dollar on one model at 8-bit precision, but loses to it (0.54 times) when the Nvidia system runs at 4-bit, which the TPU can't do natively. Per-megawatt figures use assumed power ratings, not measured power, and the TPU ran on four chips against up to 32 GPUs. Google's own claim for its eighth generation, twice the performance per watt, is a vendor figure. The MLPerf inference round of September 2026 counted 30 submitters; the organisers' summary of it names Nvidia, AMD and Intel hardware, not TPUs, Cerebras or Groq.

What an outsider can use. MLPerf's public results; JAX's Pallas for TPUs.

What the lab could test. Joules per token on one laptop across batch sizes and 8-bit versus 4-bit, with idle power subtracted: how much of an "efficiency" claim is precision, not silicon?
