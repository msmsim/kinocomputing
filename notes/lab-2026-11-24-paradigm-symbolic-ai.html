Symbolic AI is now the verifier, not the brain

Intelligence as explicit rules and logic that a machine manipulates and can check, the approach that dominated AI before neural networks.

Where it stands. The best 2026 results use symbols to check neural guesses rather than replace them. A neural prover paired with a formal proof checker, ProofEvolve, is reported at a 57.8% average solve rate across three hard maths benchmarks, with no false proofs in over 400 re-checks. An Isabelle system for the seL4 microkernel proved 77.6% of 2,794 held-out theorems against 40.3% for the standard automated tool. These are the authors' own figures on benchmark sets; we read the ProofEvolve abstract, not the full papers. The old limits (brittleness, grounding symbols in the world) are still acknowledged.

What an outsider can use. Lean and mathlib, the Z3 solver and LeanDojo are free and run on a laptop.

What the lab could test. How much of a prover's score comes from the neural model and its retry budget, and how much from the symbolic checker: same model, checker on and off, retries counted.
