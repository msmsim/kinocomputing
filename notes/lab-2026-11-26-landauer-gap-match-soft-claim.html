The brain–chatbot match to the thermal floor is soft

An essay's finding that a brain and a chatbot each spend about 10²¹ times the minimum thermodynamic energy per bit reproduces arithmetically (7 × 10²⁰ and 6 × 10²⁰ times the floor), but the two "bits" are measured differently and each input is only good to a factor of ten. We read the match as a curiosity, not evidence of a shared mechanism. This is an untested idea, checked by us only against the cited sources: the essay, Zheng and Meister, Epoch AI. Working: our note.
