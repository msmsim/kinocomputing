A DNA computer's 25-bit sum took hours, and its yield isn't stated

In a September 2026 Nature paper, a scaffolded DNA computer ran ten small programs on four binding sites at about 95% yield (relative to control mixtures) after a three-hour anneal. Its headline 25-bit addition is one harder-than-typical input that "has good completion within 14 h", with no yield figure in the main text; the same paper's simplest program, copying one bit along 25 sites, reached 59%. So the small results hold, and the scale-up is shown but not quantified. We read the paper's main text only (open access), not its supplementary notes, and no energy per computation is reported. Checked against the full text, and challenged by an adversarial review that narrowed our wording. Evidence: audit notes, paper.
