"Best at the edge of chaos" is not a reliable rule for reservoirs

Two simulation studies, a 2020 one and a July 2026 one that builds on it, find that tuning a reservoir computer to the "edge of chaos" does not reliably give its best performance: in the 2026 study, on three chaotic or quasiperiodic signals, the best setting matched none of three versions of the edge. We read the idea as a loose heuristic, not a rule. Checked by hand against the full texts of those two papers only: Carroll and Du and Wang. We did not read the original edge-of-chaos papers, and no physical reservoir was tested. An adversarial check narrowed the wording. Evidence: audit notes.
