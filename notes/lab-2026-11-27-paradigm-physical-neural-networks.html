Physical neural networks: training is the live problem

Physical neural networks run a network's layers in physics (light in a chip, resistor arrays, mechanical lattices), so the hard part is training them when their internal state can't be read or differentiated.

Where it stands. A 2025 Nature review frames the training methods. On a microring-resonator chip, sharpness-aware training reached 97% on handwritten digits against 80% for backpropagation when the chip's digital model was wrong; a 2 °C swing dropped backpropagation to 17%. A Tokyo and NTT optoelectronic system cut computation about 40%, by the authors' figure. Both report accuracy and robustness, not a full energy audit of lasers, converters and control. Elsewhere: an Israeli bistable mechanical network.

What an outsider can use. PyTorch-ONN, Neurophox and AnalogVNN simulate optical and analog nets with noise.

What the lab could test. Train a small network against a deliberately wrong digital twin; compare ordinary training, noise injection and sharpness-aware training.
